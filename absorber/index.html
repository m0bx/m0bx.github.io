    Calculation method and limitations

    Geometry

    The central backing is a square with side C. Each wing has surface length W. The wing angle α is measured from the central backing plane. In a principal-plane section, the structure span is H = C + 2W cos α. Wheel height is then added.

    The captured half-angle is evaluated from the opposite antenna phase centre to the outer edge of the backing: θ = atan[(C/2 + W cos α)/(R + d − W sin α)].

    RF estimate

    The normalized TE11 power pattern is approximated as P(θ) ∝ cosq θ. The exponent is fitted so that the entered edge angle has the entered relative level. The displayed cone-power fraction assumes rotational symmetry.

    The far-field indicator uses RFF = 2D²/λ. It is a planning check and does not replace S21 ripple, time-domain gating or movement tests.

    Construction limits

    Four rectangular wings leave diagonal corner openings. The optimizer covers the vertical and horizontal principal planes only. Add corner infill or overlapping absorber pieces if full azimuthal interception is required. Stands, antenna bodies, hinges and cable clearances are not modelled.

    Minimum block count is an area lower bound and assumes offcuts can be reused. Confirm the final cutting layout before ordering material.
